Welcome to Exploring Your New Lifestyle

This is a web-based system that will support users who value creativity in tracking their workouts through photography and videography; and participating in upcoming fitness-related trends, activities, competitions, challenges, and races whether in person, virtually, locally, or globally. It will also support users with sourcing alternate creative and fun activities that don't involve the gym that is fitness-related. The users will be able to participate in community discussions that foster engagement, connections, and motivation.
